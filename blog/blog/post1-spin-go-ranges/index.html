Spin & Go Preflop Ranges: ICM-Optimal Strategy Guide 2026

Published: June 2026 | Reading time: 8 min

Spin & Go tournaments have become one of the most profitable formats for modern poker grinders. But profitable play starts with solid preflop ranges. In this guide, we'll break down ICM-optimal opening ranges, 3-bet strategy, and position-specific charts you can use immediately.

What Are Spin & Go Ranges?

A Spin & Go range is the set of poker hands you should play (open, call, fold, 3-bet) from each position in the early stages of a 3-handed tournament. These ranges are calculated using ICM (Independent Chip Model) to maximize your expected value regardless of blind structure or opponent tendencies.

Unlike regular cash games where you can adjust based on player types, Spin & Go opponents rotate constantly. This makes balanced, GTO-informed ranges essential.

Why Preflop Ranges Matter in Spin & Go

  • You play 70-80% of hands preflop: Getting this right sets the tone for postflop profit
  • Balanced play: Opponents can't exploit you if you're not predictable
  • ICM adjustments: Your range changes as stack sizes shift
  • Speed: Pre-calculated ranges = faster decision-making = more volume

Spin & Go Preflop Ranges by Position

Small Blind (SB) Opening Ranges

The SB has the widest opening range (you're first to act and get 2:1 on a call from the BB). Standard GTO approach:

Stack Size (BB) Opening Range Hand Count
20-30 BB (Early) Top 30% (QQ+, AK, AQ, KQ, suited connectors) ~90 combos
10-15 BB (Mid) Top 40% (includes JJ, AT+, K9+, Q9+) ~120 combos
5-10 BB (Push/Fold) Top 50%+ (nearly all pairs, broadway, most aces) ~150 combos

Key point: As stacks shrink, you open wider. This is where our Spin & Go tool saves time — it calculates exact ranges for your current stack.

Button (BTN) Opening Ranges

BTN is the second-widest range. You're last to act preflop and have significant positional advantage:

  • Early stacks (20+ BB): 25% range (AA-JJ, AK, AQ, KQ, QJ, suited connectors)
  • Medium stacks (10-15 BB): 35% range (adds TT, 99, AT+, K9+, Q9+, J9+)
  • Short stacks (5-10 BB): 45% range (push-fold territory)

Cutoff (CO) Opening Ranges

CO opens tighter than BTN but wider than UTG. Standard ranges:

  • Full stacks: Top 20% (high pairs, AK, AQ)
  • Medium stacks: Top 30%
  • Short stacks: Top 40%

3-Bet Strategy in Spin & Go

3-betting is how you take control of the hand and build pots with strong holdings. In 3-handed Spin & Go, 3-bet frequency is higher than 6-max cash:

  • From SB vs BTN open: 3-bet 25-35% of hands (QQ+, AK, AQ, some medium pairs)
  • From BB vs any open: 3-bet 20-30% (premium hands + some bluffs)
  • From BTN vs CO open: 3-bet 15-20% (mostly premium)

The goal: Balance value 3-bets with bluffs so opponents can't exploit you by folding too much or calling too wide.

Common Mistakes in Spin & Go Preflop Play

  1. Playing too tight: Many new players fold marginal hands (like 88, AT) when they should be playing them. This makes you exploitable.
  2. Not adjusting for stack sizes: Your range should shift as stacks shrink. If you open the same range at 20 BB as at 5 BB, you're leaving money on the table.
  3. Overplaying weak aces: A9o, A8o are marginal in early position. You can fold these without losing edge.
  4. Ignoring ICM: Some players use cash game ranges (too loose) in Spin & Go. ICM is tighter because all-in situations are frequent.
  5. No balance: Always 3-betting with value or always folding creates exploitable patterns.
